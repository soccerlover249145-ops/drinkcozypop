What's inside.

Six things, nothing hidden. Per 12 fl oz can: ~80 calories, 4g sugar, 0mg caffeine. Contains milk.

Questions

When can I get it?

We're aiming for 2027. People on the list get in before anyone else.

How much will it cost?

A 12-Pack is $49.99, about $4.17 a can, and shop orders ship for $9.99. A Club Box is 24 cans for $99.98 with shipping included. 4-packs and a numbered Founders Box arrive at launch too. Prices aren't final yet.

How does the Cozypop Club work?

Our subscription, opening at launch. Pick a mix and get 24 cans every 4 or 8 weeks for $99.98, shipping included. Your first three boxes each carry a gift: an Everyday Cozy Cap, then a Wave coaster, then a Sticker Pop cap. Your first box has a money-back guarantee, one per household. Members get first pick of new flavors and a vote on what's next, and can swap, skip or pause anytime.

Where will it be sold?

Online first, shipped to your door. Then stores, one city at a time.

What's a Cozy Cap?

A cap that snaps onto your can, with a slide-open sip hole. Set it down, come back to it later. The Everyday cap is $12 and the sticker-covered Sticker Pop is $15. Founding members get one in their first box.

How do I move up the list?

Share your link. Each friend who joins moves you up 50 spots, and the rewards stack. See the ladder at the bottom of this page. A friend counts once they confirm their email.

Is there alcohol or caffeine in it?

Neither. Amaretto is the name of the almond flavor, and every Cozypop is non-alcoholic. Every flavor is caffeine-free too.

Is it dairy-free or vegan?

No. The protein is whey, which comes from milk. It's clear and light, but it does contain milk.

What allergens are in it?

Milk, from the whey. The almond note in Tart Cherry Amaretto comes from flavoring, and we'll confirm with our factory whether any tree nuts are involved before launch. Every can lists its allergens.

How is it sweetened?

A little real fruit, honey or cane sugar, plus monk fruit and stevia leaf extract. About 4 grams of sugar a can.

Who is it for?

Grown-ups. Cozypop is made for adults, not kids. Like any protein drink, check the label and ask your doctor if you're unsure.
