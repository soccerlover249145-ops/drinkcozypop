Privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

We collect as little as we can, we never sell your information, and you can ask us to delete it any time.

This policy covers drinkcozypop.com, our emails, and, once orders open, orders you place with Cozypop ("Cozypop", "we").

We don't ask for your birthday, and we don't buy lists.

The companies that help us

We share only what each one needs to do its job for us. They aren't allowed to use it for their own marketing.

  • Google (Gmail): receives the emails you send us.
  • Kit (kit.com): will send our emails and store the list once our sign-up form is connected.
  • A payment company such as Stripe: will take payments once orders open.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this site. Like any host, it may keep basic server logs (such as IP addresses).
  • Google Fonts: loads the fonts on our pages, which means your browser asks Google's servers for them.
  • Shipping partners: will get your name and address to deliver your order.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect people from fraud, or if Cozypop is ever sold or merged (your information would stay under this policy).

Cookies and tracking

  • Our pages use no advertising trackers and no analytics.
  • Our pages may remember small things in your own browser (like a code from a link or your quiz answer) so the page can use it.
  • If we ever add advertising pixels, we'll update this policy first and add a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link, and we'll honor the Global Privacy Control signal from your browser.

We don't sell your information

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for cross-site advertising.

Emails

  • We only email you about Cozypop.
  • Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, and you can always just reply and ask us to stop. We honor it right away (always within 10 business days).
  • You'll still get emails about an order you placed (receipts, shipping, delays).

Your choices and rights

Anyone can ask us to:

  • show you what we have about you,
  • fix something that's wrong,
  • delete your information (we may keep order and tax records the law requires us to keep),
  • stop emailing you.

California residents have rights under California law, including the rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale or sharing (we don't sell or share). We won't treat you differently for using them.

Email drinkcozypop@gmail.com with "Privacy" in the subject. We'll confirm it's you (usually by replying from the email we have) and answer within 45 days, usually much sooner.

How long we keep things

  • List sign-ups: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.
  • Orders: as long as tax and accounting rules require (often up to 7 years).

Keeping it safe

We use well-known providers, strong passwords and two-step login on our accounts. No system is perfect, but if something ever goes wrong with your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Kids

Cozypop is made for adults. Our site isn't meant for children and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. You must be 18 or older to buy. If you think a child gave us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For big changes, we'll tell the list by email.
